## Discovery principles

- Keep the README title and first paragraph descriptive and stable.
- Prefer natural language over repeated keyword lists.
- Link to the repository from relevant academic/software profiles when appropriate.
- Use issue and release titles that describe real capabilities rather than marketing claims.
- Do not market third-party AI-detector scores as a product capability or guarantee.
- Keep screenshots, roadmap, contact routes, citation metadata, and public/private boundaries current.
