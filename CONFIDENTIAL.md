# Confidentiality Notice

This repository is a **private beta proof of concept**.

## Allowed

- Review by approved collaborators and potential sponsors.
- Internal testing and feedback.
- Branch-based contribution through pull requests.
- Discussion of high-level product goals and UX.

## Not allowed without written permission

- Republishing or mirroring repository contents.
- Sharing source files, screenshots of private implementation details, or model assets outside the approved group.
- Extracting or reusing undisclosed implementation details in another product.
- Publishing benchmarks, internal roadmaps, private architecture notes, or unpublished research logic as your own.

## IP boundary

This POC intentionally omits production model weights, private prompts, internal heuristics, API credentials, and the full implementation. Access to this repository does not transfer ownership of the concept, source code, research methods, or future implementation.

For collaboration or sponsorship, use the process described in `COLLABORATION.md` and `SPONSORSHIP.md`.
