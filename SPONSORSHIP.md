# Sponsorship & Research Partnership

The project is currently a **public beta / proof of concept** for a local-first Microsoft Word research workbench. We are open to discussions with sponsors, academic partners, institutions, and technical collaborators who can help validate and mature the concept.

## Useful sponsorship and partnership areas

- Windows / Microsoft Word testing hardware
- Intel CPU/iGPU and OpenVINO optimization support
- Microsoft 365 / Office add-in testing environments
- Research software engineering time
- UI/UX design and accessibility review
- Citation/retrieval API credits for controlled testing
- Security and privacy review
- Academic pilot studies and usability evaluation
- Reproducible benchmarking and QA support

## What sponsors or partners may receive

Depending on the agreement, partners may receive:

- Early beta demonstrations
- Progress updates
- Acknowledgment in beta documentation
- Feedback sessions and roadmap discussions
- Access to agreed proof-of-concept builds
- Opportunity to support pilot evaluation
- Collaboration on defined research-software or usability studies

## What sponsorship does not automatically include

Sponsorship does **not** by itself grant:

- Ownership of the project or concept
- Rights to unpublished/private source code or model weights
- Access to credentials, private manuscripts, personal data, or unpublished research
- A commercial license to third-party components
- Permission to redistribute private beta builds

Any broader rights require a separate written agreement.

## Short project pitch

> **Word Research Workbench** explores a local-first Microsoft Word workflow that brings scholarly lookup, citation handling, research notes, integrity-oriented utilities, optional web workspaces, and CPU-friendly local text processing into one compact research environment. The beta focuses on researcher control, privacy, ordinary Windows hardware, and practical academic workflow rather than requiring a dedicated GPU.
