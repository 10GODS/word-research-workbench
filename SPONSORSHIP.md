# Sponsorship & Research Partnership

The project is currently a **private beta / proof of concept**. We are open to discussions with sponsors, research partners, and technical collaborators who can help validate and mature the concept.

## Useful sponsorship areas

- Windows/Office testing hardware
- Intel CPU/iGPU and OpenVINO optimization support
- Microsoft 365 / Office add-in testing environments
- Research software engineering time
- UI/UX design and accessibility review
- Citation/retrieval API credits for controlled testing
- Security/privacy review
- Academic pilot studies and usability evaluation

## What sponsors receive

Depending on the agreement, sponsors may receive:

- Early beta demonstrations
- Private progress updates
- Acknowledgment in beta documentation
- Feedback sessions and roadmap discussions
- Access to agreed proof-of-concept builds
- Opportunity to support pilot evaluation

## What sponsorship does not automatically include

Sponsorship does **not** by itself grant:

- Ownership of the project or concept
- Rights to private source code or model weights
- Access to credentials, private manuscripts, or unpublished research
- A commercial license to any third-party component
- Permission to redistribute the beta

Any broader rights require a separate written agreement.

## Suggested sponsor pitch

> Research Workbench explores a local-first Microsoft Word workflow that brings research browsing, scholarly lookup, citation handling, notes, and optional offline text-processing into one compact workspace. The private beta focuses on usability, privacy, and practical Windows hardware rather than requiring a dedicated GPU.

For partnership discussion, contact the repository owner through GitHub.
